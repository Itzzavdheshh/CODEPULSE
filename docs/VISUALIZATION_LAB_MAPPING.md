## 1. Product Philosophy & Information Architecture

CodePulse structures Visual Intelligence around the developer product philosophy:

$$\text{CHANGE} \longrightarrow \text{IMPACT} \longrightarrow \text{RISK} \longrightarrow \text{ACTION}$$

The navigation bar is organized into clear product sections where product capabilities lead and academic syllabus experiments serve as supporting capabilities:

```text
VISUAL INTELLIGENCE
│
├── ANALYZE (Primary Software & Data Inspection)
│   ├── Overview & Health (Compact KPI strip, health gauges, analysis map)
│   ├── Software Topology (Control Flow Graph CFG, AST Explorer, Symbol matrix)
│   ├── Data Analytics & Profiling (Dataset profiler, quality score, chart builder)
│   └── Advanced Analytics (Correlation matrix, IQR anomalies, parallel coords, linked views)
│
├── UNDERSTAND (Narrative & Provenance)
│   ├── Visual Story & Query (Guided 6-step analytical narrative + Ask Your Data)
│   └── Artifact Lineage (Dynamic cross-engine DAG provenance graph)
│
├── ACADEMIC LAB (Separated Laboratory Section)
│   └── Visualization Lab (Executable Syllabus Experiments 01 to 10)
│
└── UTILITIES (Presentation & Export)
    ├── Presentation Mode (Fullscreen slide viewer for viva & classroom demos)
    └── Export & Reports (SVG, PNG, CSV, JSON, Markdown report generation)
```

## 3. Provenance-Scoped Risk & Data Integrity

To prevent metric confusion between static analysis and machine learning predictions, CodePulse enforces strict provenance tagging across all views:

- **STATIC ANALYSIS**: Static structural risk calculated from CFG cyclomatic complexity $V(G) = E - N + 2P$. (Program $V(G) = 8 \longrightarrow \text{MODERATE Risk}$).
- **ML PREDICTION**: Risk predictions generated by fitted machine learning models (Decision Trees, Random Forest) labeled with prediction confidence.
- **DATA ANALYSIS**: Dataset quality and completeness ratings derived from measurable missingness, duplicate, and outlier ratios.
